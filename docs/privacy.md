# Privacy Policy for Unigram Mobile

**Effective date:** September 4, 2026

ZuneTracks ("we," "us," or "our") publishes Unigram Mobile, a third-party Telegram client for Windows 10 Mobile. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

## Information handled by the app

To provide Telegram messaging features, the app processes information associated with your Telegram account, including your phone number, profile information, contacts when you choose to sync them, messages, media, files, calls, settings, and information you choose to share, such as a location.

Message and media data are stored on your device and are sent to or received from Telegram's services as needed to provide the features you use. Telegram independently processes this information under its [Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy).

## Optional device permissions

The app requests device permissions only when you use the related feature:

- **Contacts** to display, synchronize, create, or annotate contacts.
- **Location** to share a location or use live location features.
- **Microphone and camera** to record voice messages, take photos or videos, or participate in calls.
- **Pictures, videos, and removable storage** to select, save, send, or receive media and files.

You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings. Some features will not work without the corresponding permission.

## Diagnostics and analytics

Release builds use Microsoft App Center Analytics and Crashes. These services may receive diagnostic and device information, app version and device-family information, and crash reports to help us understand app reliability and usage. We do not use these services to intentionally collect message content.

## Sharing of information

We share information with:

- **Telegram**, to operate Telegram messaging, calls, notifications, and related account features.
- **Microsoft App Center**, for the diagnostics and analytics described above.
- Service providers only when needed to operate the app or comply with applicable law.

We do not sell personal information.

## Data retention and security

Data cached by the app remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, clear app storage, or Telegram removes it under its own policies. We retain diagnostic information only for as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, security, and service improvement.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Protect your device and Telegram account with the available device and account security controls.

## Children

Unigram Mobile is not directed to children who are below the minimum age permitted to use Telegram in their jurisdiction. Do not use the app if you do not meet that requirement.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated policy here and revise the effective date.
